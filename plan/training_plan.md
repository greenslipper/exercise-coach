## Race Day Strategy

**Pacing:** The goal is even splits. First 5 km should feel almost embarrassingly easy — resist. Hold 4:00/km through the halfway mark. If you feel good at 30 km, begin to push. The race starts at 35 km.

**Calves/Achilles:** Warm up 10 min easy jog + drills before the start. Don't sprint the first km. If calves tighten before 30 km, drop to 4:05–4:10/km immediately — sub-2:52 still beats your PB.

**Kit:** Vaporflys. Gels every 25 min from km 10. Pre-load with carbs Fri and Sat.

---

## Key Session Guide

**Easy run:** 5:00–5:20/km. Conversational. HR < 145. The backbone of the plan — do not run these too fast.

**Tempo run:** 2 km warmup, then continuous running at 3:45–3:50/km. Comfortably hard — you could speak a few words but not a sentence. This pace develops lactate threshold. HR ~165–172.

**MP run:** Sustained running at exactly 4:00/km. Should feel controlled but purposeful. HR 155–165. The most specific session for race day.

**Progressive long run:** Run the first 70–80% easy (4:45–5:00/km), then finish at MP (4:00/km). Simulates the second half of the race on tired legs. The last 5–8 km are the key part.

**Strides:** 4–6 × 20s accelerations at ~3:20–3:30/km, with full recovery between. Not sprints — controlled speed. Used sparingly to keep neuromuscular sharpness without fatigue cost.
